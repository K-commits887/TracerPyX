## **Three important outputs**

CONFIRMED

**Vulnerable symbol identified**

Symbol is supported by patch/source evidence and passes validation.

UNCERTAIN

**Dynamic call cannot be resolved**

Potential target exists, but static evidence is insufficient.

PACKAGE_ONLY

**Advisory lacks symbol-level information**

Package is affected, but no vulnerable function can be established.

**The central idea:** Analyse each package/advisory combination once, store evidence-backed vulnerable symbols in the database, and reuse them across projects. During each project scan, TracerPyX only needs to match those symbols against the application's entry points and call graph.
